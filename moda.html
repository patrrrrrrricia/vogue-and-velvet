Frequently Asked Questions

  • What are the current fashion trends for this season?
    The current fashion trends for this season include vibrant colors, floral prints, oversized clothing, and statement accessories.
  • How can we update our wardrobe without spending a lot?
    To update our wardrobe without spending a lot, we can opt for new accessories, such as scarves, belts or jewelry, or we can reorganize and combine different clothing items we already have to create new and interesting looks.
  • What are the essential pieces we should have in our wardrobe?
    The essential pieces in our wardrobe should include a white shirt, a pair of classic jeans, a well-fitted jacket, a black dress and some versatile pairs of shoes.
  • What are the basic rules for choosing clothes based on body shape?
    The basic rules for choosing clothes based on body shape include highlighting your best features and minimizing areas of concern, selecting appropriate cuts and lengths, and balancing your body proportions.
  • How can we create a distinct personal style?
    To create a distinct personal style, we start by getting to know ourselves and understanding our tastes and preferences, exploring various styles and influences, and then adapting them to truly reflect our personality and lifestyle.
