What are the main steps for discovering your personal style?

Discovering your personal style can start with exploring your personal preferences regarding colors, textures and silhouettes, along with an analysis of the styles you are attracted to and your unique personality. Also, it is useful to draw inspiration from various sources, such as fashion magazines, online influencers or even people around you, and to experiment with different combinations of clothes to build a style that truly represents you.

Elegance versus Transient Trends
